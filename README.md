## About me

I'm a Computer Engineering student who enjoys building practical projects in **machine learning, computer vision, and autonomous systems**.

Most of my recent work has been around reinforcement learning, UAV simulation, visual perception, and applied machine learning. I like taking a problem beyond a basic notebook: building a working prototype, testing it, and documenting how it behaves and where its limits are.

Right now, I'm strengthening my machine-learning foundation while continuing to work on autonomous navigation and intelligent decision systems.
